This is a meaningful cut relative to training volume; watch strength/recovery/energy and don't wait for the scheduled check-in if training degrades.

Why a Moderate Deficit

Body recomposition (simultaneous muscle gain + fat loss) is achievable at 10 years of training experience, but slower and more execution-dependent than for a newer lifter — high protein + genuine training stimulus + only a mild deficit. This is built for modest, steady change, not a fast cut.

Calibration Protocol

  • Weigh in regularly (see Progress tab)
  • After 2–3 weeks: losing ~0.5–1%/week → on track; not moving → drop ~100–150 kcal; losing >1%/week → add ~100–150 back
  • Re-adjust every 2–3 weeks, not off single-day fluctuations
  • Override early if training performance clearly drops before the scheduled check-in
