⚠️ Lower Back Disc Constraints & Form Rules
▾
Bulging Disc Constraint: Avoid heavy loaded spinal flexion, risky conventional deadlifts, and weighted spinal flexion (e.g. weighted sit-ups, Roman chairs). Favor neutral-spine, back-friendly alternatives. Flagged exercises are marked below with ⚠️.
  • Neutral Spine Compounds: Heavy axial free-weight lifts are replaced with chest-supported/cable rows, leg press, and machine knee flexion/extension.
  • Core Safeguards: Crunches are performed unweighted on a mat; if any disc tightness occurs, swap immediately to isometric anti-extension/anti-rotation movements (e.g. planks, Pallof press).
  • Machine Calibration: Always check machine feel before loading top working weights on unfamiliar equipment.
🎯 80/20 Routine Principles & Hypertrophy Logic
▾
  • Full Body Frequency: 3 core days hitting opposing muscle groups 2–3× weekly; far more time-efficient and anabolic than single-muscle bro-splits.
  • Antagonist Supersets: Push ↔ Pull pairings share zero equipment and maximize muscular rest between working sets without adding gym downtime.
  • Inferred Working Weights: Programmed from a 90% training-max haircut on your estimated 1RM from real logged sets to safely account for intra-session fatigue.
  • Baseline Effort: Unstated sets default to ~0 RIR (near-failure), matching your real gym baseline.
  • Hyrox Conditioning: General conditioning (Zone 2 + Norwegian 4×4) is baked into the routine; Day 5 station practice is fully optional.
