## Training Philosophy: 80/20
Optimize for the 20% of work that drives 80% of the result. Favor efficient, high-yield training over volume for its own sake:
- Full body sessions over single-muscle-group splits (see `ROUTINE.md`)
- Prioritize compound, multi-joint movements over isolation work
- Cut low-value volume/accessory work that doesn't clearly move the needle on hypertrophy, leanness, or Hyrox readiness
